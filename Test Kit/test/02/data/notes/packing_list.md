# Pre-trip packing list

Trip: Lisbon, 2026-10-17 to 2026-10-24. Carry-on only this time, so the
forty-litre backpack and one zip-top bag for liquids.

## Clothes

- three quick-dry t-shirts, one long-sleeve merino top
- one mid-weight fleece (doubled as a pillow on the last red-eye)
- rain shell -- the fold-up one, not the heavy Gore-Tex
- five pairs of socks, two of them wool-blend
- slip-on shoes for airport security, plus broken-in trainers

## Toiletries and health

- TSA-approved travel bottles, re-filled and labelled
- broad-spectrum sunscreen, SPF 50, reef-safe
- blister plasters and a small first-aid kit
- re-usable water bottle, empty for security

## Electronics and documents

- USB-C charger and a short back-up cable
- e-reader pre-loaded with two long-form books
- print-outs of the hostel booking and the travel-insurance policy
- passport -- check the expiry date well in advance

Post-trip, do a proper debrief: cross off what went unused so the
next list is shorter. Last time the hand-held fan and the plug-in
kettle both came home untouched.

## Left-behind list from last time

- travel iron (hotel had one, twice)
- paperback novel (the e-reader won)
- second fleece (one was enough, even in the shoulder-season evenings)
